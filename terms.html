Code of Conduct

1. Discipline

  • Every student must follow the discipline and rules of the institute.
  • Behaving respectfully towards trainers, staff, and fellow students is mandatory.

2. Punctuality

  • Students must attend class on time.
  • Frequent lateness or unnecessary absence should be avoided.

3. ID Card

  • If the institute issues an ID Card, it must be carried during class.

4. Use of Mobile Phones

  • Mobile phones must be kept on silent mode during class.
  • Mobile phones must not be used without the trainer's permission.

5. Audio, Video & Screen Recording

  • Audio Recording, Video Recording, Screen Recording, or Live Streaming of any Lecture, Practical, Webinar, or Training Session is not allowed without the institute's written permission.

6. Study Material

  • Notes, PDFs, PLC Programs, SCADA Projects, HMI Projects, Videos, Software, and other Study Material provided by the institute must be used only for personal learning.
  • Copying, sharing, selling, or publishing any material on the internet is prohibited.

7. Practical Lab Rules

  • PLC Kits, HMI, SCADA System, computers, and other equipment must be used carefully.
  • Following the trainers' instructions is mandatory.
  • Compensation may be required for intentional damage.

8. Prohibited Behaviour

The following types of behaviour are strictly prohibited:

  • Misconduct, abusive language, or fighting
  • Threatening or harassment
  • Damaging institute property
  • Troubling other students
  • Consuming or using alcohol, tobacco, cigarettes, gutkha, or any narcotic substances on institute premises
  • Any kind of illegal activity

9. Personal Belongings

  • Students must take responsibility for safeguarding their own mobile, laptop, bag, money, documents, and other valuables.
  • The institute will not be responsible for loss, theft, or damage of personal belongings.

10. Online Classes

  • Decent language must be used during online classes.
  • Behaviour that troubles other students or trainers must be avoided.
  • Login details must not be given to anyone else.

11. Placement

  • Being present on time for interviews is required.
  • Maintaining professional behaviour in front of companies is mandatory.
  • Attempting to get placement by giving false information should not be done.

12. Violation of Rules

In case of violation of this Code of Conduct, the institute reserves the right to take any of the following actions:

  • Verbal or written warning
  • Temporary suspension
  • Exclusion from training
  • Cancellation of admission
  • Legal action if necessary

13. Changes to the Code of Conduct

The institute reserves the right to make changes to this Code of Conduct at any time as required.

14. Final Declaration

By taking admission or starting training at TECHO Industrial Automation, the student will be deemed to have read this Code of Conduct and agreed to follow it.
